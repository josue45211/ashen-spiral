# Paragon clip bake (0.25 phase D)

Generated by `Tools/paragon_clip_bake.py` from 47 sampled Paragon-on-Manny clips (`Tools/paragon_clip_import.py`, `Tools/paragon_clip_export.py`). Nothing is shipped as animation: only numeric pose curves over the armour rig's channels (`ASEnemyClipTracks.h`). **The baked clips are ON (`BakedClipsOn=true`, switched on 2026-10-19 for the playtest): the abilities in the table play their clip, every other ability its hand-made track. Set the switch to false to go back.** Abilities 20-33 (elite mutations, ward bosses) share the body language of one of the abilities below (`TrackIndex`), so they follow it. Abilities 1 (dive) and 2 (molt) pose the fliers' wings and have no clip.

Gate: the blow starts at least 0.25 s into the clip (0.15 s for the chained counter-strikes, riposte and blink strike), so the wind-up is a telegraph and not a snap, and at most 30% of curve samples are clamped to the rig's limits. The candidates are tried in order (best suited first) and the first that passes is kept; an ability with none keeps its hand-made track.

**The switch** is `BakedClipsOn` in `Source/AshenSpiral/Public/ASEnemyClipTracks.h`; both players need the same build. To keep the hand-made pose for one ability while the rest play clips, delete its `case` line from `BakedClipIndexFor`. To turn all clips off, set it to false. Offline previews of the clips: `export_enemy_poses.exe baked` (see `Tools/preview_enemy_poses.py`); they are geometry sheets, not engine captures, and the first look at them was not convincing for the ground pound (it ends in a deep hunch) and the slash (the arm reaches straight out), which is why this is a playtest question and not a default.
