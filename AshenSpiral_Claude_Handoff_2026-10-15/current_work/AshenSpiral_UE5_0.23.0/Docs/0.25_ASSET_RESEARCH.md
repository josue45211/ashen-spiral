# 0.25 asset research (2026-10-19)

Research only. Nothing was imported, converted, moved or modified: every file below was read in place under
`C:\ProgramData\Epic\EpicGamesLauncher\VaultCache` (about 22 GB, 9,100 files). Nothing was opened in the editor, so
everything about quality is from file names, sizes, FBX headers and the Fab metadata, not from looking at the assets.

## Read this first: what I could not verify

1. **Licences are not in the vault files.** The `metadata` files carry the listing text, seller, tags and an AI flag
   but no licence field. Fab's standard licence normally allows commercial use, but the exact terms (and any CC-BY
   attribution duty, or a "personal use only" listing) are shown only on each listing's page under Fab > Library.
   **Josue must confirm each listing's licence there before anything ships.** Until then every row says "unverified".
2. **AI-generated assets.** Four listings are flagged `isAiGenerated: true` by their sellers (Inferno Dragon, Warrior
   Titan, Harpy Assassin, Stonereaver Dragon; the last two say only their textures were AI-enhanced). Store disclosure rules
   (Steam asks for it) and the Fab seller terms may apply. Josue may release free or cheap, so this is worth a decision
   before any of them are used.
3. **Rig compatibility** below is inferred from file structure and names. The Paragon pack says "retargeted to Manny";
   whether that is the UE4 or UE5 Manny skeleton can only be confirmed after an import (not done, by order).
4. **Playtest input.** I used the 0.24 results Edison relayed on 2026-10-19 18:38 (no separate playtest file exists
   for 0.24; `Docs/PLAYTEST_0.23.2.md` is the older round). Several of those findings are bugs, not art problems, and
   I say so rather than force an asset onto them.

## Proposed asset-to-feature mapping for 0.25

Josue's wish list first, then what the playtest says.

- **Endless arena skin:** Dragon Cave stone kit (stoneworks pillars, stone walls, large rocks, dead trees, bridge
  pieces). The kit already spans the endless decay direction P2 filed: lush to withered to ruined, using
  dead trees and rubble for the middle stage and skulls, skeletons and inferno rocks for the blood stage.
  Circle of Death serves as a layout reference.
- **Concrete blocks for arena construction:** a downscaled concrete tile from the Concrete column applied to our
  existing procedural blocks, plus Dragon Cave `SM_StoneWall` and `SM_StoneworksStone` as variants. Keep our
  block actors and their collision; only the visual mesh changes.
- **Player model and skin:** no asset here is a drop-in. Options are Quinn, Manny or Echo from GASP. This is
  HIGH-COST (it touches the cape, armour parts, weapon attach, and the whole pose system), so it needs a
  Needs Josue decision, not a quiet swap.
- **Enemy models:** nothing in the vault is a ready enemy with the full animation set. The AI dragon and titan
  have walk/run only; the harpy and stonereaver have idle only. Parked behind P2's art direction.
- **Animations:** the Paragon pack is the only real source, and it targets Manny, which our ABP_Manny_Combat and
  the baked death curves already use. Best fit: `minionsManny` for grunts, and the Wraith, Countess, Narbash,
  Serath and Phase attack sets for the Tier 2 humanoids.
- **Anything else:** Dragon Cave coins, cup, jewels and treasure piles for chest and shop dressing; GASP foley for
  footsteps; the Molten Face Hammer for elite hammer enemies.
