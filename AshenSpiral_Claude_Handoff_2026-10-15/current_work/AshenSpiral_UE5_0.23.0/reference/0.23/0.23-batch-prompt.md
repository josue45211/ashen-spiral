## TRACK A — Animation (P2 art-directs)

### A1. Cinder Blade animation redo
The sword was judged as harshly as the hammer ("a joke"). Same treatment the hammer got in 0.22:
- Pose foundation first: correct sword grip (the 0.21 grip fix was hammer-focused).
- Then the 3-hit combo animation: fast horizontal swings, 3rd hit lunges forward (the lunge is the Blade's identity — swift, smooth, mobility weapon).
- Weight opposite to the hammer: fast wind-up, snappy strikes, minimal follow-through. It should read *fast*, not heavy.
- [NEEDS P2'S FRAMES: P2 is generating Blade reference sheets like the hammer's 10-frame set. If they arrive with this prompt, use them; if not, work from the hammer's pattern (poses → combo → feel) and the design spec, and flag the Blade as needing P2's art pass in a later round.]
- Acceptance: 3-hit combo reads clean at full speed; lunge connects visually; no joke poses.

### A2. Locomotion overhaul
Characters move stiff/robotic. Target: fluid, weighty movement — steal Helldivers 2 (P2's reference): natural acceleration/deceleration, body lean into movement, foot placement that doesn't skate.
- Applies to both players, all weapons.
- Must not change movement *speeds* or game feel numbers — this is visual only.
- Acceptance: sprinting, strafing, and direction changes look natural, not robotic.

### A3. Cape movement
The cape is a stiff board. It should flow with movement and attacks (Helldivers 2 steal).
- Reacts to locomotion (A2): streams behind when sprinting, whips on direction change, settles at idle.
- Reacts to attacks: flares on the hammer slam and the Blade lunge.
- Acceptance: cape never clips through the body visibly; motion reads as cloth, not cardboard.

### A4. Enemy animation pass
Enemies are stiff; sword enemies wrongly shoot projectiles (melee enemies should melee).
- Fluid attack/move animations for all enemy types.
- Sword enemies: melee swing attacks, not projectiles. (If a ranged enemy type needs projectiles, that's a design question — default: sword = melee.)
- Acceptance: no enemy looks robotic; sword enemies close distance and swing.

### A5. Hit reactions
- Enemies: visible hit reactions — flinch on light hits, stagger on heavy (hammer finisher, leap landing).
- Players: brief, readable feedback on taking damage (no long stunlock — roguelike fairness).
- Knockback already specced per-weapon (hammer: small/small/large) — make it read visually.
- Acceptance: every damage event has a visible reaction; heavy hits feel heavy on both ends.

## TRACK B — Systems (Josue)

### B1. Difficulty portals
Portals offering Normal / Hard / Very Hard at run start (from the direction answers).
- Must be a real choice: Very Hard meaningfully harder (enemy damage/HP scaling), Normal the current baseline.
- Rewards scale with difficulty — harder portal, better drops. [DEFAULT: +25% / +50% drop quality on Hard / Very Hard]

### B2. Fresh character slot
Second character slot so a maxed-out player can test the early game fresh.
- Fully independent progression (its own Hearth upgrades, talents, unlocks).
- Acceptance: new slot starts at zero, old slot untouched, switching works.

### B3. Boss anti-cheese
In 0.20–0.21, every boss but the first died on spawn to portal+hammer ("kill this boss on command").
- Bosses need spawn protection: brief invulnerability or a spawn animation that can't be burst through. [DEFAULT: 2s spawn protection + spawn animation]
- This is not a boss *redesign* — just close the spawn-kill exploit. Full boss rework stays deferred.
- Acceptance: no boss dies during its spawn; the portal+hammer opener is strong but not a kill.

### B4. Co-op synergies v1
Bosses were the playtest lowlight; synergies are the aimed fix.
- Implement the flagship: fire + lightning = lightning blast, bigger than the sum of its parts.
- Usable vs regular enemies, but tuned to matter most vs bosses.
- VFX + message callout when a synergy triggers ("SYNERGY: Lightning Blast") — players must *know* it happened.
- [DEFAULT: start with 3 synergies — fire+lightning, and two more of your design following the same pattern: combined > sum, callout, boss-relevant]
- Acceptance: synergies trigger reliably in co-op, the callout fires, bosses feel the difference.

### B5. Rarity rebalance
Legendaries currently feel weak — rarity must mean power. Concrete per-trait changes (decided 2026-10-15, Josue):
- **Second Dawn → Vengeance Oath** (replaces the dud): when saved from lethal damage, go invulnerable 3s and your next 3-hit finisher deals 5x. Same survival genre, stronger alternative.
- **Phoenix Oath, spiced up:** keep the cheat-death (survive lethal at 35% HP + 2s protection) and add a radial burn nova eruption on proc — it punishes whatever almost killed you. Future niche: refine further when one-shot boss mechanics arrive (revive scales with 1-hit death danger).
- **Emberforge Evolution, gated:** only triggers/is offered when the player already has at least one trait at cap — never a dead draw.
- **Echo of Choice, demoted:** fine trait, but rare or epic — not legendary-tier.
- Blood Pact stays as is (good tradeoff design).
- General rule going forward: legendaries get unique effects, not just stat bumps — build-defining, not marginal.
- Acceptance: a legendary drop is an event; players can feel the tier difference.


### B6. Well gambling
The U-shaped risk appetite: players gamble when desperate or dominant.
- At any well, stake ash for a spin — outcomes scale with the stake; big wins and total losses both possible.
- Odds tuned so it's tempting at both ends: nothing-to-lose when desperate, house-money when dominant. The middle (comfortable but not rich) should feel like the sucker's bet.
- [DEFAULT: one gamble per well per run — no farming the same well]
- Acceptance: the gamble UI is clear about stakes and possible outcomes; no exploit loops.

### B7. Boss arenas that serve the fight
No more resized wave maps. Each boss gets an arena designed around its mechanics — the rotunda reference (filed in `reference/`) is the starting point.
- P2 art-directs; arenas should telegraph the boss's identity before the fight starts.
- Arena features must interact with the boss's attack patterns (cover that matters, hazards that sync with phases).
- Acceptance: every boss arena feels bespoke; players can tell which boss lives there on sight.

### B8. Endless mode v1
The second game mode. Fully spec'd 2026-10-15 (Josue interrogation). P2 art-directs the portal and arena.

**Entry:** A sinister PORTAL (not a menu), active only after beating normal mode at least once. Bloody, foreboding — "many adventurers entered, none came back whole."

**Run start:** Fresh character, but permanent skill-tree buffs carry in.

**The arena:** One large arena, ~3x boss-arena size, LOTS of scattered obstacles. Its own visual identity (not Archives stone): the SKY tracks the reward checkpoints — clear sky for waves 1–20, slightly gloomier for 21–50, then a red bloody gloom from wave 50 on, with enemies looking and feeling faster and angrier.

**Wave flow:** Waves chain directly (stragglers get absorbed into the next wave — no hunting). Reward chest cadence: EVERY wave for 1–20, every OTHER wave for 21–50, every 3 waves after 50 (anchored 50, 53, 56, 59, 62…). At each checkpoint transition, a clear message warns: difficulty up, rewards sparser. Revisit cadence after playtesting.

**Boss waves:** Every 10 waves. Boss waves ALWAYS grant a chest on top of the cadence (59 cadence, 60 boss bonus, 62 cadence).

**Scaling:** Elites scale ~1.25x the regular rate; bosses ~1.5x the elite rate. Bosses are the main run-killers; late regular waves still lethal. Knee at ~wave 40 (starts feeling unfair); after wave 50, bosses gain one-shot mechanics — telegraphed area attacks and homing projectiles dodgeable only with a well-timed dash, and they must LOOK obviously lethal.

**Between waves:** Chest spawns → both players pick → either player presses E → next wave. No AFK timer; waits forever. (Timer only if randoms matchmaking ever exists.)

**Downed state:** A player can revive the other within a 20–30s bleed-out window. A bar above the downed character, visible to both players, shows bleed-out progress. Actively reviving PAUSES the bar (does not reset it) — if the revive isn't completed, the bar resumes.

**Arena shuffle (anti-camp):** Cover/hazard layouts reshuffle on RANDOM timing — average every 3–5 waves, range 1–7 (min 1 wave grace after a shift). After wave 50, switches to TIME-based: average every 2–3 minutes, can trigger mid-wave. The timer pauses while no enemies are out (chest phase).

**Chest:** 3 choices base per player. Skill-tree upgrades: 3→4 choices for ~150 ash, 4→5 for ~750 ash.

**Power curve:** Repeat picks at full strength up to the normal-mode cap; each 5-block past the cap steps down (~80%, ~70%…, exact curve tunable at implementation).

**Sidegrades:** After ~wave 15, up to half the chest shifts to utility/mobility/sustain. (Implementer's judgment; tune after playtest.)

**Endless-exclusive traits:** Rare stacking max-HP-per-kill (Crab Champions style), close-range kill bonuses, speed-kill rewards (kill within 1s of engaging), rare infinitely-scaling attack buffs appearing from time to time.

**Economy:** Earn ash and scrap during the run. "Lucky" shop event: ~15% chance after selecting a chest, never before wave 10. Shop stock costs scrap; very rare strong upgrades cost ash.

**Death screen:** Leaderboard (highest cleared wave) + full run stats — damage, kills, damage taken — for both players.

**Acceptance:** A full loop is playable co-op: portal entry → waves → chests → boss waves → shuffle events → lucky shop → death screen with leaderboard and stats. The wave-40 knee and wave-50 shift are felt, not just numbered.
