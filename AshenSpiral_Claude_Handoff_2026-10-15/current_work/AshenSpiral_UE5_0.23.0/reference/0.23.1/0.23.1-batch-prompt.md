## A3. Cape movement (references attached)

Player 2's cloth references are in `reference/`:
- `cape-motion-sketch.gif` — the line-art sketch: how the cape lifts, billows,
  and resettles. The silhouette language to match.
- `cape-knight-flow.mp4` — the knight on the wall: heavy cloth in wind, the way
  it catches, spreads, and falls back. The weight and drama target.

Directives:
- The cape must read as heavy cloth with weight, not a stiff plane or a
  weightless ribbon. It lags behind movement, catches on direction changes,
  and settles with a beat of delay.
- In wind/idle: the knight-video behavior — broad billow, slow resettle.
- In combat: the cape reacts to dashes, leaps, and swings (lift on the RMB leap,
  flare on dash), but never clips through the body or weapons.
- P2 owns feel and signs off.

If further visual questions arise during implementation: ask Josue and P2 ALWAYS
one question at a time. Never batch questions.

What we know: hub placement side by side (Normal → Hard → Very Hard), hover

## B8. Endless visuals (text spec — P2 refs pending, art-direct as specced)

The endless portal:
- Sinister, bloody. It must look like what it is: a place adventurers enter and
  don't come back whole. Old blood, scratch marks, trophies of the failed —
  P2's detailing instincts apply.
- Gated: only active after beating normal mode once. Inactive state should read
  as dormant/dark, not broken.

The endless arena (separate visual identity from the Archives):
- ~3x boss-arena size, lots of scattered obstacles.
- The SKY is the difficulty telegraph, in three states:
  1. Waves 1–20: clear sky.
  2. Waves 21–50: slightly gloomier.
  3. Wave 50+: red, bloody gloom — and enemies look/feel faster and angrier.
- The sky transition at wave 50 must be unmistakable. It IS the checkpoint
  message, delivered visually.
- FUTURE DIRECTION (P2, refs filed): full environmental decay, not just the sky.
  `reference/endless-env-clear-lush.png` → `reference/endless-env-gloomy-dead.png`
  → `reference/endless-env-blood-ruin.jpg`: the landscape itself dies in stages —
  lush green → withered grey wasteland → ruined castle with a river of blood.
  Current round keeps the sky-transition approach; the decay version is the
  reference for a later pass.

Constraints: these are looks only. No mechanics changes in this round — the
endless systems (B8 in 0.23) are untouched.
