## Context
Ashen Spiral, UE5, native C++. v0.21.0 shipped the melee patch: no reloads on melee, animation speed = fire rate = DPS, automatic 3-hit combos. But the hammer/sword animations landed badly ("a joke") — this round redoes the hammer completely: poses, combo animation, and a new mobility leap. Blade rework is separate; do not touch it. Do not touch ranged weapons.

## Sequencing (do in this order)
1. Pose/grip foundation (head orientation, two-handed grip) — everything else builds on this.
2. The 3-hit combo animation, mapped to the 10-frame reference.
3. The RMB leap.
4. Feel layer (hitstop, shake, debris, SFX hooks).

## 1. Pose/grip foundation
- **Head orientation fix:** the hammer's squared head must sit parallel to the swing direction/arc at every point in every animation. This was the visible bug.
- **Two-handed grip** throughout the entire combo — both hands on the haft, no one-handed moments.
- **Wind-up posture:** visible shoulder/hip coil before heavy hits (frames 6–7 of the reference). Weight reads before it lands.

## 2. LMB: 3-hit combo (automatic)
Hold LMB; hits chain automatically. Full combo must be completable by holding.

**Hit 1 — left swing**
- Horizontal left-to-right, **frontal arc** (in front of the character, NOT a 360° circle).
- Reference: 10-frame sheet, frames 1–2.
- 62 base damage, 0.72s. Small knockback. Light hitstop (~60ms), light shake.

**Hit 2 — right swing**
- Mirror: horizontal right-to-left, frontal arc.
- Reference: frames 3–5.
- 62 base damage, 0.72s. Small knockback. Light hitstop (~60ms), light shake.

**Hit 3 — planted smash (the finisher)**
- **No jump.** Overhead raise → slam straight down, feet planted.
- Reference: frames 6–10 (raise, slam, debris burst).
- 161.2 base damage (2.6x). 1.188s wind-up + slam.
- **Directional** — P2 confirmed: the finisher is a frontal smash, same as hits 1–2. No radial on the combo.
- Heavy hitstop (~140ms), heavy screenshake (respect the motion-sickness toggle), debris burst, peak SFX.
- **Commitment:** once the hit-3 wind-up starts, it cannot be canceled — the design intent is "you WANT to see it through even though it puts you in danger."
- Keeps 0.21 behavior: finisher collapses your nearest well.

**Combo rules**
- Combo window: 1.2s between hits; if it lapses, the next LMB starts a new combo at hit 1. [DEFAULT]
- Whiffing does NOT break the combo — the risk is positioning, not whiff-punish. [DEFAULT]
- Dashing cancels the combo back to hit 1. [DEFAULT]
- Attack-speed traits scale all three hit animations proportionally (animation speed = fire rate, per the 0.21 model); the combo window scales with it so fast builds don't drop combos.
- A subtle visual tell (glow/intensity ramp) as the combo progresses, so players feel hit 3 coming. [DEFAULT]

## 3. RMB: mobility leap + landing slam (melee only)
- A dash-plus: ~50% higher and a little further than a standard dash. Escape/engage tool that **hits on landing**.
- Reference: `hammer-leap-RMB-ref.png` — crouch-launch, full arc, hammer raised overhead mid-flight. The hammer lands first: **radial damage on landing, ~100 base** (between hit 2's 62 and the finisher's 161).
- Cooldown **7–8s base**, no ash cost — cooldown is the cost. (Future: rebalance the cooldown and/or add cooldown-modifying traits when traits get their rework.)
- Invulnerability from **frame 2** (the crouch-launch startup stays vulnerable) until the character is back on their feet visually. Do not change existing frame timing to fit the i-frames — fit i-frames to the frames.
- Leap cancels/resets the LMB combo to hit 1. [DEFAULT]
- Ranged weapons do NOT get this. Melee only.

## 4. G: former alt-ability
- The old right-click ability moves to G, behavior unchanged. Melee only.

## 5. Feel layer
- Hitstop: ~60 / ~60 / ~140ms across the three hits. [DEFAULT]
- Screenshake: light / light / heavy, all behind the existing motion-sickness toggle.
- Knockback: small / small / large (the Halo 3 fantasy on the finisher).
- Cape moves with the swing (part of the broader 0.22 animation work, but the hammer must not look wrong without it).

## Constraints
- Keep 0.21 numbers unless this prompt changes them: 62 / 62 / 161.2 base · 0.72 / 0.72 / 1.188s · ~108.52 full-combo DPS · ash_cost 15.
- Do not alter ranged weapons, the Cinder Blade, boss attacks, or prices.
- Co-op: both players can use the hammer; leap/Well-collapse behave per-player.
